PetPal Academy

Privacy Policy

PetPal Academy is a parent-led learning app that lets families create a pet teacher, play guided activities, and manage a subscription. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to parents.

Effective date: March 20, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect account information such as your email address and authentication identifiers when you create or sign in to an account. We also collect pet photos you choose to upload, generated pet teacher images and avatars, child setup details such as age range and selected learning goals, learning progress, and technical information needed to operate the service.

How We Use Information

We use this information to create and personalize your child's pet teacher, provide activities and progress features, sync your account across devices, process subscriptions, maintain security, and improve the app. Uploaded pet images may be sent to our AI and media processing providers to generate the pet teacher assets shown in the app.

Services We Use

We use Supabase for authentication and database storage, Cloudflare R2 for pet image and generated asset storage, RevenueCat for subscription state, Apple for in-app purchases and Sign in with Apple, Google for Sign in with Google, OpenAI and Nano Banana for AI generation features, and Replicate for image processing tasks such as background removal when enabled.

Children's Privacy

PetPal Academy is designed for parents and caregivers to set up for children. Purchases are protected by a parent gate. Parents should only upload content they have permission to share and should review the information stored in the app before allowing a child to use it.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep account and app data while your account is active so the app can work as expected. If you delete your account in the app, we delete account records, related pet teacher data, learning progress, and stored pet assets associated with that account, except where limited retention is required for billing, security, fraud prevention, backups, or legal compliance.

Your Choices

You can delete your account from inside the app. If you have an App Store subscription, you must also manage or cancel it separately in your Apple subscription settings. You may also contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information associated with your account.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@petpalacademy.com.
